# RBH Step 1 Review Button Update — 2026-10-05

Step 1 now ends with one simple decision row instead of a separate no-action section.

- **Close — no action needed** opens the existing documented closure process.
- **Continue to investigation →** acknowledges the review and advances the report to Investigation.
- The visible Case owner / investigator assignment remains removed from Step 1.
- No Supabase schema, permission, notification, closure, or workflow-status behavior was changed.
- On smaller screens the two buttons stack for easier tapping; on desktop they remain together in one decision row.

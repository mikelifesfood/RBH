Under 8 CCR §342, a qualifying event must be reported to Cal/OSHA immediately — as soon as practically possible and ordinarily no later than 8 hours after the employer knows, or with diligent inquiry would have known, of the event. Section 342 contains a limited exigent-circumstances exception. Use the official Cal/OSHA reporting instructions for the current reporting method and district-office information.

Close without corrective action

Use this when the report was reviewed and no corrective action is needed.

This report will leave the open work queues but remain in history. The closure reason and your account will be saved with the report.

Reopen record

Return a resolved safety record to active review while preserving its complete history.

The prior closure stays in the permanent history. Reopening clears the closure timestamp. A previously verified corrective action is returned for another review cycle before the record can be closed again.
  • Original report, notes, attachments, investigation, and prior evidence are preserved.
  • The previous resolved status and reopen reason are recorded in the activity history.
  • Closed corrective-action records return to corrective action with verification cleared.
  • No-action and duplicate records return to active review.

Restart workflow

Return the management workflow to Step 1 without erasing the permanent history.

This clears the current management work. Root cause, corrective-action plan, assignment, due date, priority, completion, and verification values will be reset. This action cannot be undone from the screen.
  • Original employee report and original attachments stay unchanged.
  • Cal/OSHA screening and RBH awareness time are preserved.
  • Notes, activity history, and prior corrective-action evidence are preserved.
  • A server-side snapshot of the cleared workflow is retained.

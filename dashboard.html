California safety & reporting notice. This system supports RBH’s Injury & Illness Prevention Program by documenting hazards, near misses, injuries, investigations, and corrective actions. It does not itself submit a required Cal/OSHA serious-event report or replace required injury-and-illness records. For a work-related death or a possible serious injury or illness, notify RBH management immediately. Under 8 CCR §342, a qualifying event must be reported to Cal/OSHA immediately — as soon as practically possible and ordinarily no later than 8 hours after the employer knows, or with diligent inquiry would have known, of the event. Section 342 contains a limited exigent-circumstances exception. Use the official Cal/OSHA reporting instructions for the current reporting method and district-office information.

Close report without investigation

Use this only when the report can be resolved during the initial review and no investigation is needed.

This report will leave the open work queues but remain in history. The closure reason and your account will be saved with the report.

Reopen report

Return a resolved safety report to Step 1 — Review Report while preserving its complete history.

The prior closure and completed workflow stay in the permanent history. Reopening starts a fresh active workflow at Step 1 so new information is reviewed before a new investigation or corrective action is chosen.
  • Original report, notes, original attachments, Cal/OSHA screening, and prior corrective-action evidence are preserved.
  • The previous resolved status, reopen reason, and prior workflow values remain documented in Activity.
  • The active root cause, action plan, assignment, due date, completion, and verification fields are reset.
  • Closed, no-action, and duplicate records all reopen at Step 1 — Review Report.

Restart workflow

Return the management workflow to Step 1 without erasing the permanent history.

This clears the current management work. Investigation findings, root cause, corrective-action plan, control type, assignment, due date, priority, completion, and verification values will be reset. This action cannot be undone from the screen.
  • Original employee report and original attachments stay unchanged.
  • Cal/OSHA screening, company awareness time, and any recorded notification are preserved.
  • Notes, activity history, and prior corrective-action evidence are preserved.
  • A server-side snapshot of the cleared workflow is retained.
